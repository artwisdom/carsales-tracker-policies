Privacy Policy

CarSales Tracker Pro — Andromeda Kinship

Last Updated: November 2026

Introduction

Andromeda Kinship ("we," "our," or "us") makes the CarSales Tracker Pro iPhone and iPad app (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. Our website, carsalestrackerpro.com, has its own privacy policy.

The short version:

  • Your deals stay on your device. The App has no accounts and no sync, and we don't keep a copy of your deals.
  • A few features need to send something off the device: subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat), ads on the free plan (Google AdMob), and the ATLAS assistant on Elite, which sends deal details to an AI provider only after you agree.
  • We don't sell your information, and the App doesn't track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information You Enter (Stored on Your Device)

The App stores what you enter locally on your device. This can include:

  • Customer details: names, phone numbers, and email addresses of your customers, if you enter them.
  • Vehicle & deal data: stock numbers, year, make and model, trade-ins, deal type, insurance company, sale and delivery dates, delivery appointments, gross profit, C.O.D. amounts, commission and spiff amounts, checklist status, and notes.
  • Goals and settings: monthly goals, commission rate and pay-plan settings, reminder preferences, and display preferences.
  • ATLAS conversations (Elite): your most recent ATLAS messages (up to 50). They are not shown again after the App restarts, and they are deleted when you start a new conversation or withdraw your ATLAS consent.
  • A background photo (Advanced and Elite), if you choose one. The App only receives the photo you pick. If you save a monthly recap card, the App adds that image to your photo library only when you choose to.

You don't create an account, and this information is not uploaded to us. We can't see it, and we can't recover it if it is lost. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your device backup may include the App's data. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we can't access them.

When Information Leaves Your Device

Subscriptions: Apple and RevenueCat

Purchases are made through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details, name, or Apple Account email.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. When the App starts, RevenueCat assigns it a random, anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat receives that ID together with your purchase and subscription status (for example, which plan, purchase and renewal dates, trial or offer status, store country and currency) and basic technical details such as app version, iOS version, and device model. This is used to unlock the features you paid for and to confirm an Elite subscription when you use ATLAS. It is not linked to your name or contact details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

If you installed the App from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple's AdServices framework provides a token that RevenueCat uses to tell us which ad campaign led to the install. This does not require tracking permission and does not identify you.

Subscription Offer Codes

You can redeem a subscription offer code (for example, one shared by a coworker) in the App Store or through "Have a code?" in the App, which opens Apple's redemption screen. Apple handles the redemption. Apple and RevenueCat let us see which offer applies to a subscription and how many times each code was redeemed, by country. We can't see who shared a code with you, and the person who shared it isn't told that you redeemed it.

Advertising: Google AdMob (Free Plan Only)

On the free plan, the App shows banner ads from Google AdMob. The App requests non-personalized ads only, meaning ads are not chosen based on your past activity in other apps or websites. The App does not ask for permission to track you, so iOS does not give the App your device's advertising identifier.

To show an ad, Google may still receive information such as your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device type and iOS version, app information, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. The App never sends your deals or customer information to Google. Paid plans don't show ads, and the App doesn't request ads for them. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

ATLAS AI Assistant (Elite Only)

ATLAS answers questions about your deals using an AI model provided by OpenAI. Before anything is sent for the first time, the App shows a consent screen that explains what is shared. Nothing is sent to ATLAS unless you agree. If you choose "Not now," ATLAS stays off and no deal information leaves your device for it.

When you send ATLAS a message (including a suggested question or a morning-brief question you tap), the App sends:

  • Your message and the earlier messages in the same conversation.
  • A summary of your deal data so ATLAS can answer: customer names, vehicles (year, make, model, stock number, new or pre-owned, deal type), amounts (gross profit, commission, spiffs and their notes, C.O.D.), dates (sale dates, delivery appointments and deliveries), deal status, goals, and your commission settings.

Customer phone numbers and email addresses are not included in this summary, and neither are the notes you write on deals. Anything you type into a message is sent as written, so please don't type information you don't want shared, such as Social Security numbers, credit details, or driver's license numbers.

How it travels: the App sends the request over an encrypted connection to our own proxy server, which runs on Cloudflare. The proxy checks with RevenueCat, using your anonymous app user ID, that you have an active Elite subscription, then forwards the request to OpenAI and returns the reply. You never need your own OpenAI account or API key. Our proxy does not store the content of your conversations. It keeps short-lived counters linked to the anonymous app user ID to prevent abuse, and they expire automatically within 24 hours. Cloudflare processes connection data, such as IP address, to deliver requests.

At OpenAI: OpenAI processes the request to generate a reply on our behalf. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default, and OpenAI may keep it for a limited time to monitor for abuse. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy. ATLAS data is not used for advertising.

You can stop using ATLAS at any time. Nothing is sent while you don't use it. To withdraw your consent, tap the shield button at the top of ATLAS, then "Withdraw consent": nothing more is sent, and the chat stored on your device is cleared. ATLAS asks for your consent again before it sends anything. "New conversation" also clears the chat history stored on your device.

Things You Choose to Share

Some features let you share content: the monthly recap card, "Invite a coworker," the message to a split-deal partner, and data exports. These all use the standard iOS share sheet. You choose the app and the person, and you can edit the message before it is sent. We don't receive a copy. The App never accesses your contacts.

An invite contains a link to a page on our website and does not include your name or any deal data. The invite page does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Our web host processes standard connection data, such as IP address, to deliver the page.

Dealer Ops Daily Newsletter Link (US Only)

On devices set to the United States region, Settings includes a link to Dealer Ops Daily, a free email newsletter from us. Tapping it opens our website in your browser. The link only tells the website that the visit came from the App. It does not pass any information about you or your deals. If you decide to sign up, you do it on the website, and the website's privacy policy applies.

Ratings, Reviews, and Apple Analytics

The App may ask for a rating using Apple's built-in review prompt. Apple handles the prompt, and we are not told whether you rated the App. The "Rate CarSales Tracker Pro" link in Settings opens the App Store.

The App doesn't use any analytics service. Apple gives us aggregated statistics (such as downloads and sessions) and crash reports only from people who have chosen to share analytics with app developers in their iOS settings.

Notifications

The App's notifications are created and scheduled on your device: delivery reminders, a monthly recap reminder, a reminder before a free trial ends, and, for Elite, a morning brief. Their content is generated on your device from your own data. The App asks for notification permission the first time it needs to schedule one of these, such as when you set a delivery reminder or start a free trial, and you can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features and unlock the plan you subscribe to
  • To answer your ATLAS questions, if you use ATLAS and have agreed
  • To show non-personalized ads on the free plan
  • To prevent abuse of our ATLAS proxy
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people find and use the App, so we can improve it

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information leaves your device only as described above, for the service providers that make those features work: Apple, RevenueCat, Google AdMob, Cloudflare, and OpenAI. We may also disclose information if required by law, but because we don't hold your deal data, we generally have nothing to disclose.

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: your deals and settings stay until you delete them. You can delete individual deals and spiffs in the App, and clear ATLAS chat history with "New conversation" or by withdrawing your ATLAS consent. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on the device. Copies in your device backups follow your iCloud or computer backup settings.
  • Our ATLAS proxy: no conversation content is stored. Abuse-prevention counters expire within 24 hours.
  • OpenAI: requests are kept only as long as OpenAI's API data policies allow, then deleted.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscription records are kept as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet tax and legal obligations. Because the record is tied to an anonymous ID, we usually can't tell which record is yours. If you contact us, we will try to locate and delete it using details you provide, such as an Apple order ID.
  • Google AdMob: Google keeps ad data according to its own policies.

Data Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's built-in security, such as your passcode and encryption. Information the App sends for subscriptions, ads, and ATLAS travels over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please keep your device locked.

Children's Privacy

The App is a work tool for automotive sales professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your Rights and Choices

  • View, edit, or delete your deals at any time in the App, or delete the App to remove all of its data from your device
  • Choose whether to use ATLAS; nothing is sent to it without your consent
  • Remove ads by subscribing to a paid plan
  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. Most of your information is only on your device, where you control it directly. For anything else, contact us using the details below, and we will respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
